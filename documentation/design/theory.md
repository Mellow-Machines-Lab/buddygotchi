# The theory: one Boop in every state

Updated 2026-11-22. The ideas behind how Boop behaves. It says why, not
exactly what; the what is in [screen-guide.md](screen-guide.md) and the
examples are in [animations.html](animations.html).

## The feel

Boop is one character that does three things at once. It shows the agent's
work, it stays with you, and it has feelings of its own. Idle and working are
not two modes with two sets of behavior. They are the same Boop with its
attention turned a different way: toward you when idle, toward the desk when
an agent works. You can always reach it.

## One body, three channels

Each part of Boop's body has one owner, in every state.

- **The pose says who Boop works for.** Boop owns it when idle. The agent's
  work owns it while a turn runs.
- **The rhythm and the gaze are yours.** When you type, Boop sways at your
  speed. When you speak, it leans in with a hand at its ear. When you scroll
  or move the pointer, its eyes follow.
- **The face is Boop's.** Its mood, and how it feels about a pass or a
  failure.

Because the channels do not compete, nothing has to be turned off. Going with
you rides on top of any pose, at a light size during work and at full size
when Boop has the pose to itself.

## The state picks the star

Every channel is on in every state. The state only decides which one is
large.

| State | The star |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | Boop, and Boop with you |
| Working | The work: commands, searches, edits, errors |
| Needs you | You: something waits for your answer |
| Listening | Your words to Boop |
| Asleep | Nothing |

Work stays the star while an agent works. The desk, the strip, the pose and
any held picture all belong to the work. Your rhythm is one small motion on
top of that, and it never hides or pauses the work.

## Who starts what

You can start anything at any time. Boop starts things mostly when idle.

A touch, a word to Boop, a swipe: these work the same in every state. During
work Boop answers in a small way and goes back to its pose. Its gags wait
until no turn runs. In a long working stretch it may hold up one thing of
its own now and then (a wish, an ask), as when idle. During work its body
still passes the time at its desk (a stretch, a look up at you, a sneeze, a
yawn when tired), and it feels the work: a hop when tests pass, a flinch
when something fails.

Only three things limit you: needs you, listening and sleep.

## Energy

Boop has one energy dial, and it shows in two places only: the eyelids and
the tempo. The time of day turns it, and so does a long stretch of work. In
the morning the first hello is a stretch and a yawn, and then Boop is bright.
Late, or after a long stretch, its lids are heavy and it moves slowly. It
still does the job; it droops between calls. The Mac app says the same in a
few words, so you know why it looks that way.

Energy is why Boop needs no costume. A hat or a prop tells you a fact you
already know. A sleepy Boop tells you how it is.

## Small details earn their place

A detail stays only if it passes three tests. It tells you something you
would not know otherwise. It lives on the channel that owns it. You can see
it from your chair. Anything else is noise, however charming, and Boop reads
better without it.

## The work is said once at each zoom

The work shows at three zooms, and no two places say the same one.

- **The kind of work:** the pose and the monitor's icon.
- **This call:** the strip, always a verb and what it is done to, in plain
  words: "building Boop", never a bare tool or file name.
- **Progress:** a picture Boop holds, live, fed by what Boop already hears.

A picture that stops changing is put down. No picture is better than a stale
one. Text alone never gets a card, because the strip already speaks in words.

## Size follows who it is for

If you can ignore a thing and lose nothing, it changes in place and never
rises or glows. If it is for you, it may take space, and it takes as much as
it deserves.

- Needs you takes the whole screen.
- A result worth a look is a show: a picture the agent drew, held up full
  screen.
- A finished turn with no show is a low sign in the strip's place.
- News from an agent you are not watching is a small pill.
- A chat turn that ends is only a look from Boop and "your turn" in the
  strip.

Shape says whose a thing is: round is Boop's voice, square and in a lane
color is an agent's. Light says it is for you: only those things glow.

## Several agents

The screen follows one agent at a time, and you can see that there are
others. A swipe changes agent. Boop changes it for you only for a reason you
would agree with: something needs you, a result is ready to show, or the
agent you watch has nothing to show and another does. After your own swipe,
Boop keeps your choice.

## The seams follow your own handoff

You type or speak a prompt, and Boop goes with you. You send it, and Boop
turns to the desk with the same rhythm still running. The turn ends, and
Boop turns back to you with the result. Idle to work and back is one turn of
attention, not a switch.

## Decided

These were open and are now settled. Change them here if they change.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the agent's text status card stay? | No. A held card is a live picture, or nothing |
| What can an agent show for a result? | A picture: an SVG it draws, or an image file. Boop draws the frame and the headline |
| How often does an agent show? | In every turn that used tools, to start. Tighten later |
| Does a show replace the finish? | No. The hop or flinch plays first, then the show, then it folds into the low sign |
| Can a failed turn show? | Yes. Bad news is the most useful show |
| Does a show from another agent interrupt? | Yes, once, then Boop returns. It waits while needs you or listening is up |
| Several results at once? | One at a time, in order, at least 8 s each. Pills wait their turn too |
| You type and speak together? | Speaking has the beat and the mark; the other hand types on. Who has what: the screen guide |
| Full size going-along during work? | No. Light size in every work pose |
| Does typing stir a sleeping Boop? | No |
| How does Boop show the time of day? | Energy: eyelids and tempo. No nightcap |
| What happens to the tiers, the busy and frantic typing, the copied pose? | The tiers fold into energy. The other two go |
| Where do the agent counts go? | The pips say them. The watched one's pip holds its name, on the line |
