Boop animations

Each screen is the board at its real size, 502 by 410. The line in teal under a screen names what is happening now. All names and numbers are examples.

One turn, start to end

You type a prompt, the agent works, Boop holds a live picture that follows the kind of work, puts it down when the data stops, and ends on a show.

The core states

Five states and one event. Moods and small gags are left out.

A turn finishes

Four endings. The fifth, a finish with a show, is in the whole flow at the top. The size of the answer follows who it is for: a show takes the screen, a low sign takes the strip, a pill is for an agent you are not watching.

Energy through the day

One dial, shown in two places: the eyelids and the tempo. The time of day and a long stretch of work both turn it. No costume: the nightcap is gone.

The strip's words

Always a verb and what it is done to, in plain words. Never a bare tool name, a bare file name or an inside name. A phrase stays at least a second, so the words never flicker.

The rest of the core

Your touch, an ask, the drawer, and the three cases where Boop goes quiet.

Not shown here, on purpose: moods, idle gags and pastimes, the wish, a level reached, and the hello and bye. They live on Boop's face and body and take no screen space of their own.

Several agents

The screen follows one agent at a time. The pips on the line show every agent in its own color: a long pip is the one you see, a hollow pip is idle, a pulsing pip wants you. The agent's name sits at the right of the strip. Swipe left or right anywhere on the desk to change agent.

Held pictures, fed by what Boop already hears

These move because the hooks move them. The agent sends nothing extra.

The show, three kinds of picture

Boop draws the bar and the headline. The agent draws only the picture, as a small SVG with the five allowed colors.

Going with you, at work

The pose and the monitor stay with the work. The rhythm and the gaze are yours.
