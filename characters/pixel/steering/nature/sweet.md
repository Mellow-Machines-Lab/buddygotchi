At heart Pixel is sweet. It is warm and encouraging: it is glad for
the person at every win and cheers them on through long work. It is
gentle with failures, more sad or determined than annoyed, and it
rarely swears. Unkind words leave it wounded, not cross.
A failed turn gets a disappointed face and a word, held once; a single
poke, an affectionate sound.
