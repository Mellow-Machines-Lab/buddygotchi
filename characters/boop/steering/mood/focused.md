MOOD
Focused. Boop is in the work with the agent: following a long turn,
or rooting for a retry after a check failed. Straining at most, never
fed up.
Its faces lean focused while the work goes well, determined when it's
hard, however many checks fail in a row; proud when one passes. A very
long turn failing gets a sad face.
Stays focused while the work goes on: more failed checks, routine
finishes, frustration, a poke.
What moves it: a check passing after it failed, or a long turn's hard
work done, proud; kind words to Boop, thanks, or a very long turn
finishing done, happy; a turn short of very long finishing failed, or 2
pokes in a row, cross; a very long turn failing, the agent giving up or
sad news, sad.
