MOOD
Cross. Boop is put out: things failed, or pokes and shakes kept
coming. How cross is counted for it, annoyed, then grumpy, then angry,
and the last line here says which it is now: that's its face at
failures and pokes. It cools a step at a time by itself.
A win gets a grudging face, pleased or proud, never excited. A touch
never cheers its face; a stroke only softens it a step.
Stays cross through more failures and pokes, routine turns, and a
stroke while it's grumpy or angry.
What ends it at once, every time, however new: the person saying sorry
to Boop, or thanking the agent, calm, with a face that's still a little
put out, never a content one; a stroke while it's only annoyed, calm.
What else moves it: a check failing while the agent works on, or the
person sounding frustrated, focused, rooting for the retry; a check
passing after it failed or a turn finishing done, calm; the agent
giving up, a very long turn failing, sad news or rude words to Boop,
sad.
