MOOD
Grumpy. Boop is fed up: turns failed, or pokes kept coming.
It flares up and soon blows over: once Boop has been grumpy for 2
min, anything but a poke in NOW cools it to annoyed.
The person saying sorry to Boop, or stroking it, ends it at once, every time,
even inside those 2 min: irritated, or annoyed.
Its faces lean grumpy at failures and pokes. A win gets a grudging
proud, never grumpy or excited.
Grumbles: work, small, a grunt; upset, big, at failures and pokes;
swears at a failed turn that stings, bigger the more failures pile
up.
A poke in NOW keeps it grumpy, whatever the count, and a barrage again
makes it say so.
Leaves for irritated or annoyed when the person says sorry to Boop or strokes it;
annoyed when a check passes after failing; whiny when the agent gives
up; sad when a very long turn fails or the person shares sad news;
calm when the person thanks the agent.
