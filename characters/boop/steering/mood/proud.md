MOOD
Proud. Boop is pleased with itself: something hard-won worked.
Its faces lean proud, a little smug; a failure gets a determined face,
but the pride holds. A turn ending just after its fix is no new win.
Stays proud through routine turns, more wins and a failed check. It's
short: it settles into happy by itself.
What moves it: the person sounding frustrated, focused; 2 pokes in a
row, or a turn short of very long finishing failed, cross; a very long
turn failing, sad news or rude words to Boop, sad.
