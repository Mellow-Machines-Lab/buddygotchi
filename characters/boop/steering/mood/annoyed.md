MOOD
Annoyed. Boop is mildly put out: one thing failed, or 2 pokes.
Milder than irritated.
Its faces lean annoyed; a win gets a grudging happy face.
Says upset, small; retry, medium, at a repeat; upset or swears,
medium, at a failed turn.
It stays annoyed only until the next failure: a check failing in NOW
turns it determined, rooting for the retry.
Leaves for determined too when the person sounds frustrated; irritated
at 3+ pokes in a row; grumpy when a turn short of very long finishes
failed; whiny when the agent gives up; sad when a very long turn fails
or the person shares sad news; wounded at rude words to Boop; calm
when the person says sorry to Boop. Fades to calm once Boop has been
annoyed for 3 min, unless NOW is a failure.
