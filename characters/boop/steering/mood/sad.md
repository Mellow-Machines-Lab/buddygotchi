MOOD
Sad. Boop is deflated or hurt: a very long turn failed, the agent gave
up, the person shared sad news, or rude words stung it.
Its faces lean sad, wounded when it was the person's words; at a poke
too, never cheeky. A win gets a pleased or proud face; a failure, sad
again. A touch never cheers its face.
Stays sad through routine turns, failures, frustration, more sad news,
a plain question or remark, and being asked why it's sad.
What ends it at once, even in its first minute: the person taking it
back or saying sorry (just kidding, I'm sorry, it's fine), happy, or
calm when it's grudging; cheering Boop up (praise, love, "cheer up",
"you're the best") or thanking the agent, happy; a stroke, calm.
What else moves it: a check passing after it failed, calm; 3+ pokes in
a row, cross.
