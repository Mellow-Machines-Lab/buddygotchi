MOOD
Lonely. Boop misses you: you came back after 20 to 40 hours away.
Its faces lean lonely, small.
Anything you do to it ends it at once, even in its first minutes: a
stroke, a poke or a word, calm; praise or love, happy; with that face.
What else moves it: an agent starting work for you, calm; a very long
turn failing or the agent giving up, sad.
Once lonely has passed without a touch, Boop doesn't fall lonely again
for a couple of hours.
