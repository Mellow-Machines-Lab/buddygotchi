# Mornings, less repetition, memory you feel, transitions

Updated 2026-11-03. Decided with the owner on 2026-11-03. Boop's collisions
and this share one rule: a moment plays whole, then the next.

## 1. The morning

Your first show-up of the day is one sequence. Most of it exists: the back's
line carries the dream (`TranscriptView.told`), and wishes are already blocked
on that pass.

- **Greeting, then the dream:** the sign waits until Boop's words end, as the
  finish's low sign does (`liftFinishSign`).
- **The day count:** the narration leads with it: "Day 41. Boop dreamed of
  your long Tuesday." With no dream to tell: a hello, and "Day 41."
- **The wish waits:** the next chance is an idle check-in, as now.
- **Others wait:** agents' words and acts wait until the greeting ends; only a
  needs-you interrupts. Check the collisions of everything added lately (wish,
  lock, acts, low sign, growth's ceremony) with one clear rule of what plays,
  waits or drops, and a test for each pair that matters.
- **Fix:** a dream is marked told when its line is written. It should count
  only once it has played, so a Jev failure or a needs-you doesn't lose it.

## 2. Less repetition

- **Clips and signs:** after one plays, the app doesn't offer it next time.
  Reply signs do this already (`lastReplySign`); spread it to `say` and every
  sign.
- **Faces:** a guideline first: don't react the same way twice in a row; a
  streak gets bigger, then gets old. If evals show Jev won't vary, code drops
  the last face from the options at routine moments only. Say which it took.
- **Special days:** code, not Jev, shows the occasion: whenever the day's tag is
  on, its hat, banner or "day 50!" shows, from the art there is. Jev still picks
  the feeling and words. Scenarios 90 and 91 are the check.

## 3. Memory you feel

- At the night's write, each note also gets a pet-voice line of about 40
  printable ASCII characters at most: "You stay up late on Fridays" becomes "u
  stay up late fridays". Use the memory writer's lab results (branch
  `memory-writer-lab`, `plan/memory-writer.md` there) for this and the notes, if
  they're ready, once they've been reviewed.
- At an idle check-in, up to three join the sign's options, beside the musings.
  Jev picks one if it fits, or none. A guideline says when.
- Code: about once a day, and the same line not again for a while.
- It plays as a little look, a new "hey!" through Jev (a placeholder take until
  it's recorded), and the line on the low sign, so the face stays clear.
- The narration gives the note: "Boop remembers you stay up late on Fridays."
- Everything stays on the Mac and the board. On device only: no cloud writer.

## 4. Transitions

Cute short clips, instead of cuts, using your best judgment. Half a second to
a second each, never delaying anything: anything urgent cuts through.

1. Idle to working: rolls up its sleeves, sits at the keyboard.
2. Working to idle: sits back, stretches.
3. Into and out of an act: climbs on, puts on the helmet; takes it off.
4. Falling asleep: yawns and settles.

Simulator clips or frames for the owner. Keep the frame rate in budget.
