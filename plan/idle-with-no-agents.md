# Idle with no agents

Updated 2026-11-02. Built. Decided with the owner on 2026-11-01. Small on
purpose: no new activities, no play mode, no new pastimes.

An idle check-in comes only with a session open, you at the Mac and recent
activity (`TranscriptView.checkIn`). With no session open, Jev rarely gets
a turn on its own, and the wish, musings and asks have no moment.

- **Idle check-ins no longer need an open session.** You at the Mac and
  recent activity are still needed. With no session open the gap is about
  twice the usual, to limit Jev calls. A test pins both.
- **One guideline in the steering, not an Example:** with no agents, Boop
  has time on its hands; it may muse, ask for something or doze.
- **Sleep stays as it is.** Boop still dozes off as it does now, and a touch
  wakes it.

An eval: at the Mac with no session, an idle check-in may make a wish.
